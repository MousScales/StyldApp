# Styld — Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** June 21, 2026

> **Important:** This document is provided for product and engineering use. Have qualified legal counsel review and customize it (including your legal entity name, physical address, data processing agreements, and state-specific disclosures) before publishing at `https://styldd.com/privacy` or linking it in the App.

At Styld, we build tools that help beauty and wellness professionals run and grow their businesses. We are proud to support stylists, barbers, makeup artists, nail technicians, lash artists, and related professionals (“**Professionals**”) with booking, client management, websites, communications, and payment technology (collectively, the “**Services**”).

This Privacy Policy explains how **Styld** (“**Styld**,” “**we**,” “**our**,” or “**us**”) collects, uses, discloses, and retains personal information when you use **styldd.com**, tenant sites such as **{yourname}.styldd.com**, the Styld mobile application (the “**App**”), and related products and support.

---

## Who This Policy Applies To

### Professionals (business users)

This Policy describes our practices when you register for a Styld account, subscribe, publish a site, manage clients, or otherwise use the Services as a Professional or **Account Owner**.

### End Clients (consumers who book or buy)

If you book an appointment, purchase a product, pay a deposit, upload photos, leave a review, or otherwise interact with a Professional through a Styld-powered booking page or site, **two parties may process your information**:

1. **The Professional** — generally decides why and how your information is used for the appointment or purchase (they are often the “business” or “controller” under privacy laws).
2. **Styld** — hosts the technology, processes bookings and payments, sends operational messages, and uses information as described in this Policy (often as a “service provider” / “processor” on the Professional’s behalf, and as a controller for our own operational and product purposes).

If you have questions about how a specific Professional uses your data, contact that Professional directly and review any privacy notice they provide on their site. This Policy explains **Styld’s** practices.

## 1. The Information We Collect

We collect personal information through the means below.

### 1.a Information you provide directly

We may collect information you provide when you:

- Register for or administer a Styld account;
- Subscribe through the App Store or other channels;
- Publish or edit your booking website (services, prices, hours, policies, portfolio, products, certifications, FAQs, branding);
- Manage clients, appointments, and communications;
- Connect **Styld Pay** or bank/payout details through our payment partners;
- Upload photos or videos (portfolio, style covers, booking reference images, business logo);
- Contact support or participate in surveys or promotions;
- Authorize integrations or permissions (e.g., photo library, notifications, location for Tap to Pay).

Information you provide may include:

| Category | Examples |
|----------|----------|
| **Identifiers & contact** | Name, email, phone, username, password, business name |
| **Business information** | Business type, address, service descriptions, durations, prices, hours, cancellation policies, license numbers (if provided), subdomain/site URL |
| **Client & booking data** | Client names, contact details, appointment history, notes, reminders you configure |
| **Financial & payout** | Bank account details and tax identifiers collected by **Stripe** for Connect payouts; subscription billing handled by app stores |
| **Transactional** | Subscription purchases, payment amounts, booking deposits, product orders |
| **Content you upload** | Portfolio media, site images, client booking photos (e.g., current hair or reference images when enabled), messages you send through the platform |
| **Communications** | Support chats, emails, feedback |

**End Clients** may provide name, email, phone, booking preferences, photos, payment card details (processed by our payment partners—not stored in full by Styld in free-text fields), promo codes, and review content.

### 1.b Information from other sources

We may receive information from:

- **Payment processors** (e.g., **Stripe**) — payment method tokens, transaction status, Connect account verification, fraud signals, payout history;
- **App stores** (Apple, Google) — subscription status and purchase receipts;
- **Authentication providers** (e.g., Sign in with Apple) — account identifiers permitted by your settings;
- **Other Users** — e.g., when a Professional imports or adds client contact information they already lawfully possess;
- **Identity, fraud, and compliance vendors** — to verify accounts and reduce abuse, where permitted by law;
- **Public or licensed sources** — to validate business information.

We do not knowingly collect Social Security numbers except where you voluntarily provide them for tax or payout compliance through our payment partners.

### 1.c Information we collect automatically

When you use the Services, we and our vendors may automatically collect:

| Category | Examples |
|----------|----------|
| **Device & usage** | Device type, operating system, browser, app version, language, IP address, pages/screens viewed, features used, crash logs, performance data |
| **Identifiers** | Mobile advertising identifiers (IDFA/GAID) where available and permitted; app instance IDs |
| **Location** | **Approximate** location from IP address; **precise** location only with your permission (e.g., for Tap to Pay on iPhone or fraud prevention) |
| **Cookies & similar tech** | On websites, cookies, pixels, and local storage for session management, analytics, and preferences |

We use this information for analytics, security, debugging, product improvement, and—where permitted—marketing measurement.

### 1.d Information we derive

We may infer information from what we collect—for example, your general geographic region from IP address, business category from onboarding selections, or feature usage patterns to improve the Services.

## 2. How We Use the Information We Collect

We use personal information for business and commercial purposes, including to:

- **Provide the Services** — create accounts, host sites, schedule bookings, send reminders, process payments and payouts, display analytics, and enable Tap to Pay where supported;
- **Communicate with you** — service announcements, security alerts, support responses, and—with consent or as permitted—marketing about Styld features;
- **Personalize and improve** — remember preferences, develop new features, conduct research, and fix bugs;
- **Process transactions** — subscriptions, booking payments, product orders, refunds, and chargebacks;
- **Verify identity and prevent fraud** — monitor for abuse, enforce Terms, and protect Users;
- **Comply with law** — respond to legal process, tax reporting obligations through payment partners, and regulatory requests;
- **Aggregate and de-identify** — create statistics that do not identify individuals.

**Professionals:** You are responsible for ensuring you have a lawful basis to upload client data and to send marketing or reminder messages through Styld, including obtaining required consents under **TCPA**, **CAN-SPAM**, and similar laws.

**End Clients:** We use your information to complete bookings and payments you request, send transactional messages related to your appointment or order, and for purposes described in this Policy.

If you provide a professional license or government ID for verification, we use it for identity verification, fraud prevention, compliance, and enforcement of our agreements—not for unrelated marketing.

## 4. Cookies, Analytics, and Advertising

We and our partners may use cookies, pixels, SDKs, and similar technologies on our marketing site and, where applicable, logged-in web experiences to:

- Understand usage and improve the Services;
- Measure advertising effectiveness;
- Provide relevant Styld marketing on third-party platforms.

Depending on your jurisdiction, some of these activities may be considered “**sale**,” “**sharing**,” or “**targeted advertising**” under state privacy laws.

**Your choices:**

- Adjust browser settings to block or delete cookies (some features may not work properly);
- Use industry opt-out tools such as [Digital Advertising Alliance](https://www.aboutads.info/choices) where applicable;
- Enable **Global Privacy Control (GPC)** or similar legally recognized signals on supported pages;
- Opt out of promotional emails via unsubscribe links;
- Limit ad tracking in iOS/Android device settings.

When we publish a live privacy rights page at **https://styldd.com/privacy**, we will provide a “Your Privacy Rights” link for cookie and targeted advertising opt-outs consistent with applicable law.

## 6. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as needed to provide the Services, fulfill the purposes described in this Policy, and comply with legal obligations (tax, payment records, dispute resolution).
